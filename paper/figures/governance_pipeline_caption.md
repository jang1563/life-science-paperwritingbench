# Figure 1 Caption

Figure source: `paper/figures/governance_pipeline.svg`.

Life-Science PaperWritingBench treats biomedical section-writing evaluation as
a governance stack before it treats it as a model-scoring problem. Source
papers pass through scientific, packaging, and integrity qualification; curated
evidence units are frozen into truth manifests; benchmark units receive release
tiers and split-safety/canary controls; only then are task bundles exposed to
single-pass or agentic model-generation lanes. The current `v0.1 research
preview` includes deterministic scoring `v2`, rubric judge `v3`, matrix
summaries, redacted canary probes, and readiness gates. Human validation is
structurally prepared but intentionally held.
