# Life-Science PaperWritingBench v0.1 Research Preview Notes

## Status

This is a `v0.1 research preview`.

It demonstrates the current benchmark governance stack, three
evidence-grounded writing families, deterministic and LLM-judge evaluation,
agentic baselines, partial hosted-frontier model coverage, redacted canary
probes, and a frozen human-validation batch.

It is not a final benchmark release, not a human-validated leaderboard, and not
a publication-grade validation claim.

## What This Preview Includes

- governance-first paper qualification, evidence extraction, task bundling,
  release-tier assignment, split safety, canary policy, and scoring layers
- writing families:
  - `methods_to_text`
  - `results_to_text`
  - `abstract_from_evidence`
- deterministic scoring `v2`
- rubric-based judge `v3`
- single-pass and agentic `writer -> critic -> reviser` baselines
- conservative selected-output policy for agentic runs
- partial hosted-frontier matrix results across DeepSeek, OpenAI, and Anthropic
  submitters / judges
- redacted completion-style canary probe summaries
- Inspect-compatible task adapter and artifact-backed replay helpers
- frozen `60`-bundle publication-validation batch with reviewer packets,
  reviewer forms, adjudication shells, and structural hold audit

## Release Claim Boundary

Safe wording:

- "research preview"
- "partial hosted matrix"
- "structurally ready human-validation batch"
- "no exact canary reproduction observed in the completed hosted-working-set
  probe"
- "human agreement metrics pending"
- "final leaderboard blocked"

Avoid wording that implies:

- human-validated benchmark status
- final leaderboard readiness
- completed Gemini coverage
- registry-complete contamination clearance
- hosted/open-weight equivalence
